Enneagram Leadership Mastermind

A 12-month small-group curriculum for leaders ready to move beyond personality awareness into genuine transformation. Four phases take you from self-discovery through interpersonal mastery to integrated leadership.

12 Sessions · 90 Minutes Each · Small Group Format
Phase 1 Self-Awareness
Session 01

Your Type Is a Starting Line

Discover your type's seeking/avoiding pattern and how it drives leadership behavior.

Session 02

Where Your Attention Goes

Explore how your type's attention bias shapes what you notice and miss as a leader.

Session 03

Your Leadership Style Under the Microscope

Examine your default leadership style and its impact on those you lead.

Session 04

What Stress Does to Your Leadership

Understand stress patterns by type and build strategies for leading under pressure.

Phase 2 Interpersonal Skill
Session 05

The Art of Listening That Changes People

Learn type-aware listening skills that transform conversations and relationships.

Session 06

Reading the Room

Develop the ability to recognize and adapt to the motivations of other types.

Session 07

Saying What You Need Without Starting a War

Master direct communication that respects type differences and reduces defensiveness.

Session 08

Navigating Conflict Across Types

Build conflict resolution strategies tailored to enneagram dynamics.

Phase 3 Deepening
Session 09

Leading the Team You Actually Have

Apply Centers of Intelligence to understand and address team imbalances.

Session 10

The Tensions That Make or Break a Team

Work with enneagram polarities to harness creative tension in teams.

Session 11

Giving Feedback That Lands

Deliver feedback calibrated to type so it is heard, not just said.

Phase 4 Integration
Session 12

Leading Beyond Your Type

Integrate 12 months of growth into a sustainable leadership practice.
